Figure 4. (a) Contrastive learning in one picture: the green diagonal holds the true pairs, and every other cell is a mismatch the model learns to score low. (b) RSTPReid has about five photos per person, so random batches would sometimes call a correct match "wrong". Building batches from unique people removes that noise. (c) Matryoshka training puts the most important information in the first numbers of the vector. (e) Almost all of the gain comes in the first epoch, and the last two epochs polish it.
